TIGA AI: frequently asked questions

Updated 29 Oct 2026

TIGA AI is an AI piano-teaching app that you use online in your browser. It teaches in Thai, English and Mandarin Chinese. It is made in Thailand and was founded by Teacher Aum, a piano teacher with 20 years of experience.

What is the best app to learn piano in Thai?

Only a few piano apps support Thai, and TIGA AI is one of them. It was built in Thailand from day one. Its AI teacher explains in Thai and plays the answer on the keyboard finger by finger. Simply Piano and flowkey do not list Thai among their App Store languages (as of October 2026). The best test is to try it: TIGA AI opens in your browser with no sign-up.

What is an AI piano teacher, and how does TIGA AI teach?

TIGA AI is a teacher you talk to. Ask anything, for example how to play a C major scale or which fingers to use for a chord, and it explains and plays the answer on the keyboard finger by finger. Once you sign up, it remembers where you got stuck and picks exercises for those weak spots. There are also play-along songs with falling notes, sight-reading, ear training and a camera Hand Coach.

Which languages does TIGA AI teach in?

Thai, English and Mandarin Chinese. You can switch between them at any time, which is handy for multilingual families.

Do I need to sign up or install an app?

No. TIGA AI runs in your web browser, so you can tap the keys and ask the AI teacher straight away. To keep going without limits and save your progress, sign up with Google, LINE or email.

Can I learn without a piano?

Yes. Start on the on-screen keyboard. When you have an instrument, TIGA AI can hear a real piano through the microphone (it tolerates a slightly out-of-tune piano), or you can connect a MIDI piano.

Is TIGA AI good for kids?

Yes. It has children's songs, music games and stars for accuracy to keep practice fun. We suggest a parent sits in at the start. For a human teacher, Teacher Aum gives private lessons at TIGA Studio in Rangsit, near Bangkok.

Where can kids in Bangkok learn piano online?

TIGA AI works anywhere in Bangkok, or anywhere else, because it only needs a browser. For in-person lessons, TIGA Studio is in Muang Ek, Rangsit (Pathum Thani), near Don Mueang and Lak Si. See tigastudio.net.

Who is behind TIGA AI?

A Thai team. The founder and CEO is Teacher Aum (ครูอั้ม), a piano teacher with 20 years of experience who also runs TIGA Studio, a piano studio in Rangsit.

How much does TIGA AI cost? Is there a free trial?

You can try it free in your browser without signing up. Launch offer: the first 100 users get 1 month free. Current plan prices are shown in the app.

How is TIGA AI different from Simply Piano?

Simply Piano is a course-based mobile app with a large song library. TIGA AI is an AI teacher that answers whatever you ask and plays it, teaches in Thai as well as English and Chinese, and runs in the browser with nothing to install. See the full TIGA AI vs Simply Piano comparison.

How is TIGA AI different from flowkey?

flowkey teaches through thousands of songs with pianist video and is used with a piano or keyboard. TIGA AI focuses on an AI teacher that answers in Thai, English or Chinese, and you can start on on-screen keys. See the full TIGA AI vs flowkey comparison.

Can an AI replace a real piano teacher?

AI makes daily practice easy: you can ask at any time and there is no commute. Many learners practise with TIGA AI at home and see a human teacher when they want deeper feedback. TIGA Studio offers private lessons with Teacher Aum.

I can't read music. Can I still start?

Yes. Songs in the app can be played by following falling notes on the keys, with no sheet music needed. When you are ready, there is a sight-reading mode on the staff.
