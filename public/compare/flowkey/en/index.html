TIGA AI vs flowkey

Updated 29 Oct 2026

Short answer: flowkey shines for learning songs. It has thousands of arrangements by professional pianists, with video and sheet music on one screen, and suits learners who already own a piano or keyboard. TIGA AI shines as a teacher. You can ask it anything in Thai, English or Mandarin and it plays the answer finger by finger, in the browser, even before you own a piano.

Who TIGA AI suits

  • Learners who want lessons in Thai, or families that mix Thai, English and Chinese
  • Beginners who don't own a piano yet and want to start on screen
  • People who want theory questions (scales, chords) answered and played on the keys at once
  • Learners who like having a real teacher available as a next step

Who flowkey suits

  • People who already have a piano or keyboard and want a big song library
  • Visual learners who like watching a pianist's hands next to the sheet music
  • Players who want Wait Mode, looping and one-hand practice

Key differences

  • Language: TIGA AI teaches in Thai directly; flowkey's App Store language list does not include Thai. Both offer Chinese.
  • Teaching style: flowkey teaches through songs and video; TIGA AI is a teacher you talk to, which explains and demonstrates whatever you ask.
  • Instrument: TIGA AI can start on on-screen keys; flowkey is designed to be used with a piano or keyboard.
  • Where flowkey is strong: thousands of professional arrangements, and the company says over 15 million people have learned with it.

Common questions

Does flowkey support Thai?

As of October 2026, flowkey's App Store page lists English and 10 other languages, and Thai is not among them. TIGA AI teaches in Thai directly.

Do I need a piano for flowkey?

flowkey's official listing says you need the app, a device and an instrument. If you don't have a piano yet, TIGA AI lets you start on on-screen keys in the browser.

Can I use both?

Yes. Practise favourite songs in flowkey and ask TIGA AI about anything you don't understand.
