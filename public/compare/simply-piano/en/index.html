TIGA AI vs Simply Piano

Updated 29 Oct 2026

Short answer: choose TIGA AI if you want an AI piano teacher you can ask anything, that plays the answer finger by finger, teaches in Thai, English or Mandarin, and runs in your browser with nothing to install. Choose Simply Piano if you want a polished, course-based mobile app with a large library of popular and classical songs and you are comfortable in one of its supported languages.

Who TIGA AI suits

  • Learners who want lessons in Thai, or families that mix Thai, English and Chinese
  • People who keep asking "why?" and want to see the answer played on real keys: scales, chords, triads
  • Anyone without a piano yet, or who would rather not install an app
  • Learners who like having a real teacher available as a next step

Who Simply Piano suits

  • Learners who want a ready-made course path on phone or tablet
  • Players who want popular hits, Disney songs and classical pieces from a big library
  • People comfortable in English or another of the app's listed languages

Key differences

  • Language: TIGA AI teaches in Thai directly; Simply Piano's App Store language list does not include Thai. Both offer Chinese.
  • Teaching style: Simply Piano guides you through courses; TIGA AI is a teacher you talk to, which explains and demonstrates whatever you ask.
  • Getting started: TIGA AI opens in the browser and the keys work immediately; Simply Piano is installed from an app store.
  • Where Simply Piano is strong: a long track record, an App Store Editors' Choice, and a 4.7 average from about 860,000 ratings (US App Store).

Common questions

Does Simply Piano support Thai?

As of October 2026, Simply Piano's App Store page lists English and 13 other languages, and Thai is not among them. TIGA AI teaches in Thai directly.

Can I use both apps?

Yes. Some learners practise songs in a course app and use TIGA AI to ask about anything they don't understand, and see it played finger by finger.

Can I start without a piano?

Yes, with either. TIGA AI has on-screen keys in the browser; Simply Piano has on-screen Touch Courses.
