TIGA ALPHA

About TIGA ALPHA

TIGA ALPHA is an AI piano teaching app made in Thailand. It teaches piano online in 10 languages: Thai, English, Mandarin, Arabic, Japanese, Russian, Spanish, French, Hindi and Korean. Teacher Aum (ครูอั้ม / Aum 老师) is the founder and CEO, and a piano teacher with 20 years of experience. TIGA Studio is the in-person piano studio. Premium is free for 7 days, then US$24.99/month.

关于 TIGA ALPHA

TIGA ALPHA 是泰国团队打造的 AI 钢琴教学 App，可用 10 种语言在线学钢琴：泰语、英语、普通话、阿拉伯语、日语、俄语、西班牙语、法语、印地语和韩语。创始人兼 CEO 是 Aum 老师（ครูอั้ม），拥有 20 年钢琴教学经验。线下钢琴教室是 TIGA Studio。高级版免费 7 天，之后每月 ¥176。
