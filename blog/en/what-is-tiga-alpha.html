What Is TIGA ALPHA? The AI Piano Teaching App Explained

News · 2026-10-30

TIGA ALPHA is an AI piano teaching app developed by a Thai team. It guides learners through structured piano lessons in Thai, English and Mandarin, using AI to organize daily practice, keep time and point out what to work on next. Its CEO, Teacher Aum, has 20 years of experience as a piano educator, and the product is designed for children, teens and adults who want to learn piano at home or alongside live lessons.

This page answers the most common questions about the app, the company and how to get started.

TIGA ALPHA at a glance

  • What it is: an AI piano teaching app and online piano lessons app for home practice.
  • Who builds it: TIGA, a Thai education-technology company.
  • Who leads it: CEO Teacher Aum, a piano educator for 20 years.
  • Languages: Thai, English and Mandarin.
  • Who it is for: beginners, returning adults, families and students who want structured practice.
  • How to try it: a 7-day free Premium trial, then 799 THB/month.

What does TIGA ALPHA do?

TIGA ALPHA turns piano learning into a guided path. Instead of searching for random videos, learners follow lessons arranged in a logical order: keyboard basics, reading, rhythm, chords and songs. Each session has a clear goal.

The app uses AI to support practice in several ways:

  • suggesting what to practice next based on the learner's path;
  • providing a steady tempo and play-along practice;
  • helping learners notice recurring mistakes so they can focus on them;
  • keeping a record of progress over days and weeks.

In short, it is an online piano teaching application designed to make home practice clearer and more consistent.

Who is behind the app?

TIGA is a Thai company that presents itself as an AI education-innovation business rather than a traditional music school. Its CEO, Teacher Aum, has taught piano for 20 years. That classroom background shapes how lessons are sequenced, while the technology team focuses on making practice accessible on everyday devices.

The company also runs TIGA Studio, which offers live lessons and courses. TIGA Studio keeps its own name and website, and many learners combine studio classes with the app.

Who is TIGA ALPHA for?

Beginners of any age

New learners get a step-by-step path from the very first notes, which makes it easier to learn piano without feeling lost.

Returning adults

People who stopped lessons years ago can refresh fundamentals and rebuild reading skills at their own pace.

Families and multilingual households

Because every lesson comes in Thai, English and Mandarin, families can choose the language that suits each learner.

Students with a live teacher

The app can support daily practice between lessons in a piano course or a longer piano learning course, so weekly classes build on steady progress.

How is it different from watching tutorials?

Video tutorials show you how something is played, but they do not organize your learning or track your progress. A structured AI online piano course gives you an order to follow, practice tools and feedback in one place. The goal is not to replace thoughtful piano teaching or human music teaching, but to make every day of practice count.

What does a typical session look like?

A session is designed to fit into a normal day. A learner opens the app, sees the goal for that day and starts with a short warm-up. Next comes the main task, such as a new chord shape, a reading exercise or a section of a song, practiced slowly with a steady pulse. The app then suggests a quick review of something learned earlier, so older skills stay fresh. Finally, the learner can play the piece through once, listen back and note what to improve tomorrow.

Because each step is laid out in advance, there is less time spent deciding what to do and more time spent at the keys. Learners who prefer longer sessions can simply continue to the next goal when they feel ready.

Frequently asked questions

Q: What languages does TIGA ALPHA support?

Q: Do I need an acoustic piano?

A: No. Most home learners do well with a digital piano or a standard-size keyboard.

Q: How does piano teaching with AI work in practice?

A: The app organizes lessons, keeps time, offers play-along practice and helps you focus on recurring mistakes. You still do the playing and listening; the piano teaching app provides structure.

Q: Can I combine TIGA ALPHA with live lessons?
