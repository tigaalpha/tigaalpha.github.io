Practise piano 15 minutes a day and actually improve: 5 methods that work

Practice tips · 2026-10-30

Practising piano every day but feeling stuck? The problem is rarely too little time. It is usually practising the wrong thing: playing the whole piece from the start again and again when you only slip in two bars. Here are five methods that fit into 15 minutes a day.

1. Slow first, fast later

Play slowly enough that you make no mistakes, then speed up a little at a time. Playing fast before you are accurate trains your hands to remember the mistake. In the app you can practise below normal speed, for example in Play Along, where the song speed is adjustable.

2. Repeat only the bars you missed

Do not play the whole song every time. Find the bar you slipped on and repeat just that until it passes three times in a row. After a round, TIGA ALPHA's practice mode shows which notes you miss most and offers a drill for exactly those.

3. Train your ear alongside

Pianists who can hear what a sound is learn new songs much faster. Spend two or three minutes in Ear Gym listening to intervals and chords, and guess before you check.

4. Play one song you love, every day

If you only drill technique you will get bored and quit. Save the last couple of minutes for music you really want to play. The app has more than 10,000 songs and Play Along makes practice feel like a game.

5. Have something tell you what to practise next

The biggest problem of practising alone is not knowing what to work on. TIGA ALPHA's AI Daily Mentor looks at your actual practice data and tells you where your weak spots are, what to do today and for how long.

A 15-minute plan to try today

  • 3 min: warm up with a slow scale
  • 6 min: repeat the missed bars (methods 1 and 2)
  • 3 min: ear training or note reading
  • 3 min: play a song you love

Most important: short practice every day beats one long session a week.
